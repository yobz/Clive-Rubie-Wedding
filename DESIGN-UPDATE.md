# Rose heirloom redesign

The published site now allows anyone with the link to view it without signing in. Search-engine indexing is discouraged through metadata; this is not access control. RSVP submissions remain server-side, and no response-reading endpoint is exposed.

The supplied 45/25/20/8/2 ratio guides visual emphasis, rather than acting as a literal pixel quota: blush and soft pink carry the narrative, grey frames the invitation and RSVP, ivory supports reading, sage anchors the attire section, and gold is limited to fine accents. Deep rose is extended with #8D3451 for readable small text and buttons. Dusty rose remains an accent, since it lacks enough contrast for small text on every pale surface.

Pinyon Script is used for the couple's names and brief handwritten captions. Bodoni Moda gives headings the character of a formal printed invitation; Inter keeps practical details readable. These are contemporary type choices intended to evoke an heirloom invitation, not claims of historically indigenous Filipino typefaces.
